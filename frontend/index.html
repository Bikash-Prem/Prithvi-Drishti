Eight agents,
one event bus

Each agent owns one job and publishes what it finds; the others react. Safety decisions — alert severity, phase thresholds — are always computed deterministically. The language model explains, it never decides.

Sentinel

every 15 min

Watches river discharge and scores it against the seasonal baseline to catch anomalies early.

GLOF

direct bypass

Monitors glacial lakes for dam failure and can alert directly, skipping the queue.

Flood Predict

ensemble

Routes a rainfall ensemble through the basin and reports probability with uncertainty bounds.

Urban Risk

per zone

Maps the forecast onto streets and districts: who is exposed, and how badly.

Alert

deterministic

Turns severity into warnings and exports them as standard CAP 1.2 messages.

Resource

logistics

Pre-positions boats, shelters and supplies before the peak, and justifies each order.

Disease Risk

post-flood

Estimates outbreak risk in the days after the water recedes.

Compound Event

fusion

Spots hazards that coincide — flood plus landslide plus outbreak — and scores the combined threat.
